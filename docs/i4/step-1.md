# I4 step 1: the plan's schema 2 and the schema-1 lift

Roadmap row: I4 step 1. Decision: [ADR-0013](../decisions/ADR-0013-the-building.md) §1, §2, §5, §7, §11, §12 (it
amends [ADR-0010](../decisions/ADR-0010-world-generator-and-world-plan.md), which stays as it is).

## What was built

- **Schema 2** (`rv/world/world_plan.hpp`, `plan_json.hpp`): the plan's `floors` (id, order) and, per room, `kind`
  (`console`, `shared`, `arcade`), `floor`, `sections`, `placement` (x and z in mm, yaw in half turns),
  `generatorVersion` and the reserved `expands`. The plan's `generatorVersion` is the highest of its rooms'. Only schema
  2 is written; schema 1 is read as its content. The reader checks what a room says of itself: its kind is its id's
  form, a console room holds exactly its own system, a shared room stands on the floor its id names, `expands` is there
  exactly for an expansion and names its head. `roomContentDigest()` is the SHA-256 of a room's canonical
  sub-document, computed and never stored. New unplaced reason `room-kept`.
- **Room ids** (`ids.hpp`): `room:<system>`, `room:<system>:<n>` (n from 2), `area:shared:<floor>[:<n>]`,
  `area:arcade[:<n>]`. `RoomId::forSystem(system)` still gives `room:snes`, so its seed and bytes are unchanged. Seeds
  derive from the id's text; `scripts/world_seed_oracle.py` covers the new forms.
- **Building data** (`building.hpp`): `assets/world/building.json` (the nine floors in elevator order, the layout
  constants) and `assets/world/systems.json` (the systems the tests and the lift use, with ADR-0013's families, the
  defaults per kind, a fallback) with strict readers; `classify()` (own row, platform-tag fallback, `other`,
  `excluded`); the floor layout as pure functions (`placeEnteringRoom`, `followDepth`, `floorRows`, `roomToFloor`).
- **The lift** (`lift.hpp`): `lift(v1, building, systems)`, pure: the v1 room becomes the console room of its system
  on its floor at the north row's first position, with the ground floor.
- **Generator 1.3** (`generation.hpp`): `generateBuilding()` plans N systems, each room generated by I3's room-level
  rules (`detail::generateRoomContent`), entering their floors in (floor order, systems.json order, name);
  `generate()` is the building of one system, so the I3 room suites run unchanged.
- **The update per room** (`incremental.hpp`): `updateBuilding()` lifts a schema-1 plan and updates each room that has
  a selection exactly as ADR-0010 §10 did. World-level checks (seed, preset, generator major, random algorithm,
  title-key rule) stay global. A room is kept when its template version, its prefab levels or its generator major
  changed: its contents are untouched, and every game of its selection that it does not place becomes `room-kept`.
  `update()` is the one-system form.
- **Validation** (`validation.hpp`, 34 rules): new hard rules `ground-floor`, `floor-unknown`, `rooms-overlap` and
  `system-rooms` (each system in exactly one room, the arcade room on the ground floor). New soft rule `room-kept`. A
  kept room's template version and prefab slots are not judged.
- **CLI**: `worldplan generate | update` plan the building. `--system` and `--selection` are repeatable
  (`ArgParser::values`). `show` prints floors and rooms, and `where-is` names the floor. `show`, `check`, `where-is`
  and `update` lift a schema-1 plan. Reference: [cli.md](../reference/cli.md).
- **App**: it lifts a stored schema-1 plan when it reads it. It updates its SNES room through `updateBuilding`, with
  each room's data read under its recorded family (`loadRoomData`). The first update writes schema 2 and backs up the
  v1 file. The room is still shown in its own frame: floors in the app are step 5a's.

## Decisions

- **The floor frame.**
  - The origin is on the hall's centre line at its west end, where the elevator stands.
  - Rows start at `rowStartXMm` (3.0 m).
  - A room's footprint on its floor is its interior grown by `wallAllowanceMm` (150 mm, the template's wall). This
    lives in building.json, so that the lift needs no template.
  - The north row has yaw 0. Its x is `rowEnd - (minX - wall)`, its z is `-hall/2 - (maxZ + wall)`. The south row
    mirrors these.
  - The lifted SNES room stands at x 7650, z -6650.
- **Platforms.** `platforms/<system>.json` is optional: a system without one takes its family and sign from
  systems.json and its theme pack, console and plastics from the kind's defaults. `PlatformManifest::console` is
  optional. Without a console, the hero corner stands without its console part (arcade, computer, other).
- **A full floor** (12 rooms per row on both rows): every game of the system is unplaced as `capacity`, naming
  `room:<system>`. `room-unknown` accepts such entries.
- **Rooms without a selection** are left as they are by the update. The `inputDigest` hashes the rooms that were given
  a selection, so it remains provenance only.
- **The update's `--system`** restricts the rooms updated and is always allowed. `generate --system` restricts the
  building, but only with `--plan` or when no plan is stored; with `--replace` over a stored plan it is a usage error.
  Selection files name the building's systems.
- **Goldens.**
  - The plan goldens were rewritten with `RV_UPDATE_GOLDEN=1`, 28 files. Each changes only in `schemaVersion`,
    `generatorVersion`, `inputDigest`, `floors` and the room's new fields: 3 lines out and 6 in per file. No fixture,
    placement, dressing, light or unplaced entry moved.
  - One golden building was added, `building-5-systems-seed-102938.json`.
  - The scene goldens did not change.
  - `tests/fixtures/world/v1/` is untouched.
  - The room goldens G01 to G10 match 0 pixels apart in Release. Their JSON files still record the plan digests of
    schema 1, which their test only warns about. Step 5a renders them again on `--start-room` and records the new
    digests.

## Deviations and limits

- **Families.** Only `cartridge_home_16bit` exists until step 2.
  - systems.json names the other families already, so their systems' rooms cannot be generated yet.
  - `worldplan generate` without `--system` leaves them out with a note, and so it does for excluded systems and for
    systems without a placeable title (ADR-0013 §3).
  - `generateBuilding()` itself gives a room to every selection it is given, so the I3 suites keep their 0-title rooms.
- **`worldplan check`.** It judges a room of an older template version or of another generator major as kept. A room
  whose prefab levels changed still fails `prefab-slots` there, although the update keeps such a room.
- **The app** updates only the SNES room. In a building made by the CLI, the other rooms stay as they are, and the
  app's `inputDigest` names only the rooms it updated.
