## Acceptable performance

Performance must be acceptable, not maximal (decided October 20, 2026). Gates and tests check these limits only, in
Release at 1920 x 1080 on the reference machine; finer figures (GPU passes, CPU sections, GPU-free benchmarks) are
reported for information and never fail a gate. The hidden `[.bench]` cases run on demand (`./scripts/gate.ps1 -Gate B
-Benchmarks`, informative rows). I3's tighter budgets are kept below as its history.

| Measure | Acceptable |
|---|---|
| Frame time in a populated room (the benchmark path, `--no-vsync`) | p50 at most 16.7 ms (60 FPS typically), p99 at most 33.3 ms (never below 30 FPS) |
| Frames while loading covers, refreshing or animating | p99 at most 50 ms |
| From the start to the room, and to the quick catalog's first page (NFR-01) | 5 s |
| Covers resident after the room shows | cold 30 s, warm 3 s |
| GPU memory | all textures at most 512 MiB |
| Catalog queries (NFR-02) | p95 at most 100 ms |
| Video | the first frame within 2 s of the selection |
| Per-frame CPU work checked by smoke tests | loosely, at the median (cover uploads 4 ms, collection planning 1 ms) |
| An elevator ride (ADR-0013 §13) | the view back within 3 s warm and 10 s cold; the frames behind the fade exempt |
| The room that holds the walker on a floor | covers resident within 30 s cold and 3 s warm; the rest of the floor informative |
| An edit shown (a move, a swap, a fixture moved) | 0.25 s, the frames within the 50 ms p99 row |
| The impact preview of a regeneration | a room 2 s, a floor and the world 15 s on the reference library |
| A save, a regeneration's commit or an undo | the room shown again within 3 s warm |
| Frame time with two clips playing (the benchmark path past a secondary TV) | p50 at most 16.7 ms, p99 at most 33.3 ms |
| GPU memory with the screens playing (the busiest floor, two slots; the viewer's clip) | all textures at most 512 MiB |
| A clip's sound against its picture | within 80 ms at p95 |
| Frame time in VR, a populated room, both eyes at the runtime's recommended size (ADR-0017 §9) | p50 at most 13.9 ms, p99 at most 20 ms, at most 1% of the refreshes missed (72 Hz); 90 Hz informative |
| Frame time in VR with a game captured on the CRT (the "in game" profile) | the same limits |

Correctness is never relaxed: determinism, NFR-08's stability, the read-only snapshots of the real library and the
focus rules stay exact.

## Working method (from I4)

Lighter than I2 and I3 (decided October 20, 2026):

- **Steps**: each step delivers a feature that can be seen or tried, with unit tests for its logic and, for what draws,
  a GPU smoke case. No step exists only to measure.
- **Notes**: `docs/<iteration>/step-<n>.md` stays short: what was built, the decisions, the deviations, the follow-ups;
  figures only where they decided something.
- **Performance**: only the acceptable limits above; no per-pass or per-section budgets, no reruns on a quiet machine.
- **Goldens**: few, for the views that define the look (at most one per new kind of room); regenerated when the look
  changes on purpose.
- **The real library**: its `[real-library]` tests run when a step touches the catalog, the import or the real rooms,
  not at every step; its read-only snapshots stay.
- **Gates**: one script run (the build matrix, the tests, the goldens, the acceptable performance) under the focus
  watcher; then the user tries the result and declares the milestone done. No signed checklist.
- **Parallel worktrees** only for steps that are truly independent; otherwise one step at a time.
- **Documentation**: `CLAUDE.md` holds the essentials, the reference is in `docs/reference/`; the architecture and the
  ADRs change when a decision does.

## I3: M2 SNES vertical slice (done)

M2 Done (specification §22): a real room generated from the data shows the cartridges of the system and stays stable
after an incremental import. Gate B (style §27, phase B): the room already looks like the concept although it is only
one room, built from the real data of an ES-DE library, with cartridges filled procedurally, a TV playing a local video,
the pickup and inspection of a cartridge, media and object fallbacks, the Balanced graphics profile and a benchmark; and
the WorldPlan snapshot is deterministic. The decisions are in
[ADR-0010](decisions/ADR-0010-world-generator-and-world-plan.md) (the generator and the WorldPlan),
[ADR-0011](decisions/ADR-0011-collection-rendering-and-cover-textures.md) (collection rendering and cover textures) and
[ADR-0012](decisions/ADR-0012-interaction-in-the-world.md) (picking, focus, inspection, the card, launching from the
world, the hero CRT); the facts behind them come from three read-only surveys of 2026-10-18: the documents
(requirements, the Gate B checklist, twenty conflicts between the documents), the code's readiness, and the reference
SNES collection (7,797 games; 1,535 with a cover, the scraped ones; 890 distinct titles among them, 727 of them USA
releases once deduplicated, 57 Europe only, 106 other, which step 2's ranks read as 760 USA or World, 57 Europe and 73
other; covers opaque 680 x 497 PNGs, 815 MiB; clips H.264 640 x 480 at 59.94 fps, already 4:3).
Decided before planning (the user and the orchestrator) and recorded in the ADRs:

- **One cartridge per title** among the SNES games that have media (890 of the 1,535 with a cover), the USA release
  preferred, then Europe, then other regions, by a defined title key and a deterministic choice; the other versions,
  hacks and unscraped games stay launchable from the quick catalog and are listed in the plan's `unplaced` with their
  reason (ADR-0010 §3).
- **Spines plus display cases, a compact room**: most cartridges stand spine-out and dense, the title on the spine; a
  few dozen stand face-out where the eye goes, chosen in priority order (favourites, then play count, then rating, then
  title): the 44 slots of the display cases and the featured wall shelf, and one face-out band at eye height across the
  hero wall's two modules (12 slots); the shelving is sized to the collection, as few spine-out modules as the titles
  and the reserve need, all full, the remaining sockets and walls given to decoration (shelf dressing, posters, the
  template's furniture), never empty or extra modules, so that the room never looks sparse; a library that fits the
  display slots and the band (G02's 50 titles) is all face-out; pointing at a spine-out cartridge pulls it out and
  shows its cover (ADR-0010 §3, ADR-0012 §3). On the reference library 56 titles stand face-out and 834 spine-out, in
  the two hero-wall modules and three side-wall modules.
- **Deterministic and incremental display**: a new title always enters the spine-out reserve next to its alphabetical
  predecessor and never reshuffles what stands; the face-out set changes only with an explicit regeneration (ADR-0010
  §3, §10).
- The **WorldPlan is a canonical JSON file** under `<user data>/worlds/<library id>/`; no database migration (schema v1
  closed at Gate M1); where-is is computed from the plan (ADR-0010 §9).
- Seeds and stable hashes use **SHA-256 of `rv_core`**, no new hash library; **no EnTT**; **no downloaded HDRI or
  texture**: the ambient light stays hemispheric with the ambient occlusion, and the Balanced profile of style §4.3 is
  the renderer as it is (one shadowed key light, ambient occlusion, sober bloom); Low and High are I8's.
- **One video decoder** in I3, SelectedGame on the hero CRT with the fallback chain to the cover and a test pattern; the

- The user's covers and cartridge images are their own media, read at runtime, never in assets, fixtures or goldens:

### Gate B

`scripts/gate.ps1 -Gate B` runs Gate A's build matrix (no warning), the unit tests (the world's snapshot goldens and
property suites among them) and the GPU smoke tests in Debug and Release, the `[.bench]` cases and the benchmark in
Release, the goldens G01, G02, G02s, G06, G07, G08 (a and b) and G10 in both, prints the summary table (logs in
`build/gate-b`) and exits 1 when a step failed; all of it under `scripts/focus_watch.py`, as Gate M1 runs.
`-RealLibrary` adds, with ES-DE closed, `RV_LAUNCH_ALLOWLIST` set and empty, and snapshots before and after through
`scripts/real-library.ps1`, the real SNES room (the plan twice with one SHA-256, an incremental update after a second
import with 0 moves, covers cold and warm, the benchmark path), the refresh's p99 and NFR-01 with the room, and fails on
any difference. `docs/testing/gate-b.md` holds the figures, the map of requirements to proofs, style §24's minimum
result ("no interpenetrating shelf, console visible, video playable or correct fallback, room readable and navigable by
controller") and style §29's first expected result, the style §26.2 checklist answered for the goldens and for the real
room, and the manual session (the controller checklist of the approved plan's §7): pad only, in the real SNES room, a
title found in a display case and one on a spine-out shelf, pulled out, inspected, launched in RetroArch and back in
the same pose, the hero CRT playing its clip, "Mostra nella stanza" from the quick catalog, "Aggiorna libreria" with
nothing changed (0 moves), the media viewer; then the card's actions with keyboard and mouse. The script
passed and the user declared M2 done on October 20, 2026 (the manual session unsigned, kept as a guide).
